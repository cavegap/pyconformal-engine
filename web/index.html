What does a conformal map preserve?

Start with vectors, then compare curved paths and their tangent images. Discover why a nonzero derivative matters and what changes at a critical point.

  1. Move the vectors in Group P
  2. Compare curves and tangents
  3. Recover a house with a logarithm
  4. Work through exercises E1–E5
Apply

Solve a simpler boundary problem.

The Schwarz–Christoffel square connects complex analysis to harmonic fields. Explore an exact thermal solution and ideal models for impedance and capacitive coupling.

  1. Map the half-plane to a square
  2. Follow temperature through the map
  3. Study a 50 Ω coplanar guide and a capacitive sensor
  4. Work through exercises E6–E7
Map the Moon

Follow a contour from sphere to plane.

Trace the same point on a lunar globe, in Mercator coordinates and under a chosen transformation. Then explore the two hemispheres, cuts and repeated poles in Peirce’s projection.

  1. Follow the thesis contour
  2. Switch views in the Peirce atlas
  3. Read the archival recovery and registration notes
  4. Work through exercises E8–E10

The demonstrations accompany Implementación en software de aplicaciones conformes, by C. A. Vega Penagos and H. A. Olivero Zapata, Universidad del Tolima (2015). New reconstructions and applications are identified separately from the original results.
