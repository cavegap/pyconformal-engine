The exponential map w = ez

Drag P or use the sliders. A horizontal line maps to a segment of a ray. A vertical line maps to a circle over one full turn.

Exponential map

f(x + iy) = exp(x)(cos y + i sin y); f′(z) = exp z
f(z + 2πi) = f(z); local length scale = exp(Re z)

The derivative never vanishes. The map is locally conformal everywhere, and becomes one-to-one when restricted to a suitable open horizontal strip.

Notation, domains and normalization

Formula and object example

w = exp(x + iy) = eˣ(cos y + i sin y); f′(z) = exp z never vanishes. Local angles are preserved, but points separated by 2πi have the same image. Horizontal lines become rays and vertical lines become circles. See a logarithm recover a recognizable house from an exponential deformation.
