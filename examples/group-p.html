Angle preservation in Group P

θ = arccos(⟨x,y⟩/(‖x‖ ‖y‖)); x = A − O, y = B − O
Rθ = [[cos θ, −sin θ], [sin θ, cos θ]]; Dλ = λI, λ ≠ 0

This is the unsigned angle between two nonzero vectors. Reflection preserves that magnitude and reverses orientation. Its geometric axis angle α corresponds to the thesis matrix parameter 2α.

Rotation and positive dilation preserve angles and orientation. Reflection preserves the unsigned angle and reverses orientation: it is anti-conformal. A collapsed ray has no defined angle.

All panels share the same coordinate extent. The common view fits the triangles automatically; it stays fixed while you drag and refits on release. Lengths and angle measurements update as you drag.
