Move an angle through Group P

Drag O, A or B in the source plane. The same vertices and two rays move in every panel. The angle at O is measured between OA and OB.

Thesis §1.2, Definition 1 (p. 12): cos θ = ⟨x,y⟩/(‖x‖‖y‖). Here x = A − O and y = B − O.

Rotation and positive dilation preserve angles and orientation. Reflection preserves the unsigned angle and reverses orientation: it is anti-conformal. A collapsed ray has no defined angle.

The same axis scale is used in all panels. Lengths and angle measurements update as you drag. Keyboard alternative: click the source canvas to focus, press O, A or B to choose a point and use arrow keys to move it.
