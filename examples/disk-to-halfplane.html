From disk to half-plane

w = i(1 − z)/(1 + z)

Drag P or use the sliders. Move to the unit circle to follow its image along the real axis.

Formula and reference points

w = i(1 − z)/(1 + z), with Im w = (1 − |z|²)/|1 + z|². Thus the open unit disk maps into the upper half-plane. Reference points: 0 → i, i → 1, −i → −1, 1 → 0; −1 is the pole. Compare an interior point, a boundary point and the pole with the controls below.
