A square plate with varying edge temperatures

This second SC problem prescribes a linear temperature profile on each of the four edges. Opposite corners are hot; the other two are cold. Follow the same point from the half-plane to the square.

Map: w = u + iv = 1 + i − (2i/κ)RF(z + 1, z, z − 1), κ = RF(0,1,2). Temperature: T(w) = 60 + 40uv °C; T(z) = T(f(z)).

What boundary problem is solved?

Steady conduction in a homogeneous, isotropic, two-dimensional plate with constant conductivity, no internal heat generation and prescribed temperatures on all four edges. In normalized coordinates: top T(u,1) = 60 + 40u; bottom T(u,−1) = 60 − 40u; right T(1,v) = 60 + 40v; left T(−1,v) = 60 − 40v.

Since ∂²T/∂u² + ∂²T/∂v² = 0, this is the exact harmonic solution. Conformal pullback preserves Laplace's equation in the open half-plane. The center is a 60 °C saddle with zero gradient. Heat flux is q = −k∇T; physical units require conductivity and a length scale. This teaching problem is a new extension, not a thermal experiment from the thesis.
