Square map and critical point

f(z) = z²; f′(z) = 2z; local length scale = 2|z|

Local angles are preserved for z ≠ 0. At the origin the first derivative vanishes; outgoing directions are governed by the quadratic term.

How to explore this example

  1. Move P

    Click or drag in the highlighted source, or use Re P and Im P. P′ is its image under z².

  2. Read the colors

    The two colored UT outlines correspond across both panels. The short rays show local directions; θ is the source opening and θ′ the image opening.

  3. Try the origin

    Move P to zero. The opening doubles there as a limit of outgoing rays; it is not a nonzero image derivative.

The two plots have different coordinate scales. Compare the numerical |2z|, not the screen lengths of the rays.
