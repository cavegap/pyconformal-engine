How to explore this example

  1. Follow one point

    Click near the cyan outline in any view or use the position slider. The selection snaps to the nearest existing sample.

  2. Compare the chain

    The globe gives context, Mercator is the source tracing, and the third panel shows its chosen transform. A–D are matching reference vertices; P′ marks the transformed selection.

  3. Switch the map

    Choose square, cube, logarithm or Peirce without changing the selected sample. Zoom to tracing is available in Peirce mode.

The historical registration is approximate. The cyan outline is an archival reconstruction, not a surveyed crater boundary. Zoom changes the view, not the mathematical coordinates.
