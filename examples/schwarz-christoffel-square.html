The transformation and a physical example

w = 1 + i − (2i/κ) RF(z + 1, z, z − 1), κ = RF(0, 1, 2) ≈ 1.311028777. The analytic branch is taken in Im z > 0, with boundary limits on the real axis. RF is Carlson’s symmetric elliptic integral.

Corner correspondence: −1 → −1 + i; 0 → −1 − i; 1 → 1 − i; ∞ → 1 + i. The interior reference i maps to the center 0.
